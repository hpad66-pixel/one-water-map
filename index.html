How It Is All Contained

Three containers on GitHub, one master and two published editions, with the pictures drawn once and appearing everywhere. Verified pushed and clean as of August 1, 2026.

AssetMaster of recordWhere it also appears
Your dictations, verbatimhardeep-soul/outputs/book-evidence/book-one/Nowhere else, by design. Private raw material.
The 12 chapter sourceshardeep-soul/book/chapters/Copied into the-steersman-book/chapters/ at compile.

The compiled book (HTML + PDF)hardeep-soul/outputs/book-compiled/the-steersman-book/index.html + PDF, the gallery, and the Pages render.
The white paper v1.2hardeep-soul/outputs/whitepaper/one-water-knowledge-graph/index.html, and the gallery.
The ledgers (TOC, continuity)hardeep-soul/book/Regenerated by scripts/build_book_index.py; never hand-edited.

The rule that keeps this clean is one sentence: everything is authored in the shipyard, and the other containers only ever receive copies. Edits happen in hardeep-soul, the compile script rebuilds the book, the copies flow downward into the reading edition, and a commit and push seals each state forever. Nothing flows upward, so there is never a question of which version is true.
